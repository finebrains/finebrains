Rack-scale AI inference, delivered as one appliance

fineBrains builds turnkey AI inference appliances for small and medium businesses — the GPU compute, the serving layer, and the cooling in a single rack, ready to run on standard facility power.

Most AI hardware is sold at two extremes: desktop boxes that top out quickly, and bare eight-GPU servers that still leave the buyer to assemble a working system around them. fineBrains fills the gap in between. Each appliance pairs an 8× NVIDIA H200 GPU node with CPU compute blades that handle tokenization and request serving, so the GPUs spend their time on inference instead of overhead.

The whole rack is air-cooled — no chilled-water loop, no coolant distribution unit, no plumbing. It is sized to real power budgets: the starter configuration runs at about 19 kW, within reach of a standard 20 kW service.
